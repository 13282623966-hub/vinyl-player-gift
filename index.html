To 仲夏夜

A birthday gift.
For the one who hears music in the spaces between stars —
each cover here a tiny stage for a memory.

May these songs travel lightly through your days,
like pages turned by a gentle summer breeze.
From past lives to new dawns,
the record keeps turning — and we, likewise, said goodbye.

FEBRUARY 21, 1925

A public-domain cover from the golden age of print.
